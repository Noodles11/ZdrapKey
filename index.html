How it works

Drag to scratch. Every layer hides one seam — expose it, then tap the star to break the layer regardless of what's left. Every 10th layer is reinforced: trace the hidden channel end to end instead. Uncovering a coin, fragment or tool never collects it by itself — a pulsing ring shows once it's exposed, and only tapping it actually loots it.

The tool wheel only ever shows what you can actually carry this run. Tapping a tool toggles it — carry several at once and their effects multiply into one blended tool, named as a portmanteau of every component (Chisel + Drill + Probe → "Chidriobe") with a chip counting how many are stacked. Any special ability or quirk any one of them has applies to the whole combo. Picking the Bare Hand always clears the combo; picking a real tool always drops the hand first.

Leftover crust you never touched is forgotten once you move on — nothing carries over unless you scratch a layer away completely. Mines and live circuits end the run and forfeit every tool found this run plus all unbanked currency. Bail between layers to keep everything. Every mine hides under a red warning aura wider than the mine itself, so you'll see red before you reach the core — it shrinks the deeper you go.

Uncovering a mine arms a 5-second fuse instead of killing you outright — it pulses faster as time runs out. Hold your thumb exactly on it (no offset here) for 3 uninterrupted seconds to defuse it; lift early and the progress resets while the fuse keeps burning. Run out of time and it blasts a crater into the crust and flings any nearby loot outward.

Live circuits draw right through the crust from the start — no digging needed to see them. Each one cycles energized (bright, dangerous) and dormant (dim, safe) on its own random timer; scratching it while thin only ends the run if it's currently energized. A hidden off-switch is buried somewhere on every layer with a circuit — dig it up and hold your thumb exactly on it for 3 seconds to permanently kill every circuit on the layer.

Natural ground — Topsoil, Sand, Clay, Gravel — sometimes hides small bugs, buried under the crust like any other signal, only showing where you've dug. Tap one to clear it, or leave it be — it's not a trap either way. Clear enough of them and you'll permanently earn the Dissolving Spray and a brand-new kind of layer to dig through.

Scissors cut a thin line like any tool. On paper-like crust — Cardboard, so far — closing that line into a loop pops the whole inside out instantly, loot included. On anything else the loop trick just doesn't work: they're a plain blade there.

Meteorite layers, out in the deep cycle, have no crust to scratch at all — just a solid shell of triangular plates, and only the Cold Chisel or Core Drill can bite into one. Tap a plate to break it into four smaller ones with a gap between them; those loose pieces drift and bounce off each other until a tap on one small enough pops it away for good. Swiping only shoves loose pieces around — it never breaks anything. The seam turns up once the plate that used to sit over it has been popped away.

The Shop below spends banked currency on a loadout for your next run only — skip charges, a coin multiplier, a tool rental, or (once you've defused 10 mines) a Second Wind that evades death once. Everything bought is spent the moment that run starts, whatever happens in it.
